Solmar City — an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6. Drive and steal cars across a sun-soaked coastal city, dodge the Solmar PD under a five-star wanted level, and run five story jobs for Mama Rosa and Vince, from a first cab fare to the Big Score. On foot and by car, with traffic, pedestrians, police chases, combat, a minimap HUD, synthesized sound and music, and a save that keeps your progress. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Play with keyboard and mouse: WASD to move and drive, Shift to run, E to enter or steal a car, F to punch, 1/2 to switch weapons, click to shoot, Space for the handbrake, M to mute, P to pause. Your progress saves automatically when a job ends.
